# Cybersecurity write-ups

Practical investigations and educational walkthroughs focused on **how evidence is examined and interpreted**. Each article documents tools, reproducible steps, screenshots, useful technical theory, and what can reasonably be concluded from the available evidence.

| **Dragonfly — Investigating a phishing email** | Outlook MSG, MAPI/OLE, email headers and authentication, HTML attachments, hashes, Windows shortcuts | [Read the investigation](dragonfly/README.md) |

The first case study is based on the [CyberDefenders Dragonfly lab (T1598.002)](https://cyberdefenders.org/blueteam-ctf-challenges/t1598002-dragonfly/), completed in an isolated training environment.

## Approach

- **Evidence first:** findings come from the lab artifacts and documented investigation steps.
- **Use the appropriate tool:** prefer straightforward methods when they provide adequate evidence; use specialised tools when needed.
- **Explain the theory:** define new concepts close to where they become relevant.
- **Be precise about conclusions:** distinguish observed behaviour from assumptions and potential follow-up analysis.

These are learning-focused write-ups from simulated exercises, not reports of production incidents. No malicious sample files are distributed.
